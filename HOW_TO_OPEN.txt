CAPACITY CONNECT — Digital Capacity Building & Learning Management Portal
Frontend-Only Prototype
========================================================================

HOW TO RUN:
1. Double-click "index.html" in this folder.
2. It will open instantly in your default web browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Brave, Safari, Opera).

NO SETUP REQUIRED:
- No Node.js or npm needed
- No Python or server needed
- No database or backend needed
- 100% self-contained frontend application with built-in mock data!

FEATURES INCLUDED:
- Modern Landing Page with Statistics, Announcements, and Featured Courses
- 1-Click Role Switcher (Trainee, Trainer, Admin) in the top navigation
- Trainee Dashboard & Competency Passport
- Course Catalog & Module Details with simulated enrollment
- Interactive Timed MCQ Assessment with instant grading & explanations
- AI Assessment Generator (Trainer Feature)
- AI Skill Gap Analyzer with 4-stage competency pipeline
- Feedback Sentiment Analysis Analytics Dashboard (72% Positive / 18% Neutral / 10% Negative)
- QR Verified Digital Certificates with live verification modal
- Gamification, Badges, XP & Leaderboard
- Admin Dashboard with Trainee Approvals & System Analytics
- Full Light / Dark Mode toggle
